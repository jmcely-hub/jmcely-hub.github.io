Teaching

Principles of Ecology (graduate course)

Graduate Teaching Instructor · Department of Ecology and Evolutionary Biology, University of Michigan

I have been the Graduate Teaching Instructor of Principles of Ecology, a graduate course for incoming PhD and master's students taught by Professor Anshuman Swain at the University of Michigan. In addition to taking part in this course for three consecutive years, I have led and guided graduate students as they navigate through it.

Although I have not yet been a teaching assistant for an undergraduate course, I look forward to it as an opportunity to learn how to communicate knowledge more effectively in an undergraduate-level class.

Dissertation proposal seminar · Department of Ecology and Evolutionary Biology, University of Michigan · 2025
